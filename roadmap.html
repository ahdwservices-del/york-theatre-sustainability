Green theatres roadmap

The road to sustainable theatres

42 actions. Two lanes. Three horizons. Published case examples for 41 of the 42 actions. Start with the easy wins and keep score as you go.

1

Pick

Start in the green Easy Wins band. Pick one or two actions per lane, open the case examples, and put a name and target date against them.

2

Track

Each action moves from Plan to Doing to Done. Add the date and one line on what happens next.

3

Log

Record each step in the progress log at the end. Small entries add up to evidence for your next review.

Individual & team = you, your list or your theatre team can start without new infrastructure. System changes = needs procurement, pharmacy, estates, IPC, catering or organisation-level decisions.
★ Big hitter = high climate impact per unit or per case (potent gases, or well-documented waste). This is an indicative flag; check against your own usage data. Ease ratings are a first-pass judgement. Re-sort anything that is easier or harder where you work.
