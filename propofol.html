Plan Your Propofol

TIVA is greener than gas. But we often draw up more propofol than we need, and the rest gets binned.

How to reduce propofol waste

  1. 1

    Estimate your dose

    Use dosing calculators to plan ahead.

    SIMTIVA
    Open SimTIVA ↗simtiva.app
  2. 2

    Draw up as you go

    Keep vials and syringes ready, but only prepare what you need.

  3. 3

    Choose the right vial size

    If a case is nearing completion, a 20 ml vial may be preferable to a 50 ml vial.

Dispose of leftovers correctly

Empty and part-used propofol vials, and syringes with the needle removed, go in the blue-lid pharmaceutical bin. Never pour propofol down the sink. Check any item in the Which bin? finder.
